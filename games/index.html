Null Parallax

A space survival game about a probe drifting between the stars. You build and repair the ship, and land on planets made fresh for each star. You cook and grow food to stay alive, mine big deposits and build a base on the ground, under a sky of real stars.

Unzip it and double-click Start Null Parallax. The game runs in your browser, only on your computer. On Windows the zip carries everything it needs. On Mac and Linux it needs Node.js 18 or newer, a free program; if you do not have it, the start file tells you where to get it. The first start needs the internet. Your saves stay in your browser for the local address and port; the README explains how to keep an older save. Every file and its sha256 are in SHA256SUMS. All rights reserved: see LICENSE.txt inside each zip.

Known rough edges: animals on planets do not move yet, you cannot steer left or right during descent, lava does not glow at night, and mining uses the suit cutter (no laser upgrade yet).
