Rooms

Get the Lobby. Then add rooms, one at a time.

SandboxVille comes in two parts. The Lobby is the base game: your own facility, with a Lobby and a Security Office. Rooms are add-ons, like DLC for a game. Each room does one job, and each one is its own small download. Every picture below is a real screenshot from the game.

Step 1

Get the Lobby: the base game.

One zip for your computer. Inside is everything that runs SandboxVille, plus the Lobby and the Security Office. Unzip it anywhere and double-click Start SandboxVille. Your browser opens by itself, already paired, and you spawn in your Lobby. SandboxVille needs Node.js, a free program. If you do not have it, the start file tells you where to get it.

Step 2

Pick rooms to add.

Each room is one add-on, like a DLC. You add only the ones you want. Put the zip in the add-rooms-here folder inside your SandboxVille folder, just as it is. No unzipping and no restart. Then walk to a glowing EMPTY LOT, press E and pick the room. SandboxVille checks the zip's fingerprint, shows you the room's full door sign, and adds nothing until you press PLACE.

What the door sign means

You read the sign before the room goes in.

Every room carries its own list of what it is allowed to do. SandboxVille turns that list into the door sign you see above, before you place the room, and again at its door. A room that can reach past its own glass box says so on a red line. Everything a room does after that shows up on the Security Office wall.
