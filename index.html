01 · How it works

Doors declare it.
The broker keeps it that way.

Every room in SandboxVille is a separate, locked sandbox. Nothing it can touch is a secret and nothing it can't touch is negotiable.

01 · DOOR

Every room posts what it can touch, in plain language, right on its door.

02 · BROKER

A capability broker grants only what the door declares. Nothing more gets through.

03 · LOG

Every attempt is recorded: room, action, and whether it was allowed or refused.

02 · Rooms so far

It is early.
These rooms already work.

They run today on our own test machine, each in its own sandbox. Public release comes after an outside security review.

ROOM 01 · THE FLAGSHIP

The Heritage Jar

A terrarium where a real FlyWire feeding circuit decides when the fly eats. Its walking is a simple rule, for now. New species evolve while the jar is open, and the jar keeps their family tree.

CAN TOUCH: its own jar data. Nothing outside it.
ROOM 02

The Desk

Your watchlist and paper trades on the wall, forecasts graded in the open.

CAN TOUCH: your paper book, read only. Never trades.
ROOM 03

The Library

Read-only access to a folder you pick, shelved and searchable.

CAN TOUCH: the one folder you chose. Nothing else.
ROOM 04

Security Test Room

A room built to be broken into. It goes after the network, storage, and the broker itself. Every attempt is watched, logged, and refused, in plain sight.

CAN TOUCH: its own small storage box. Nothing else. That is the point.
ROOM 05

File Test Room

A second test room that goes after file access: path tricks, device names, a real junction escape, write attempts. Every one is refused and logged.

CAN TOUCH: one test folder, read only (.md and .txt), and its own small storage.

03 · Security

We attacked our own rooms.
Every attempt was blocked.

In our own test suites, 51 of 51 break-in attempts were blocked: 27 against the network, storage, and the broker, and 24 against file access. Each suite also checks that the one thing its room is allowed to do still works, so the locks are real and not just broken. Every room runs in its own sandbox, and the tests check that one room cannot pose as another or reach its data.

04 · Walk the town

Walk the town in 3D.
Door by door.

SandboxVille is a 3D building you walk through in first person, or view from above as a floor plan. Walk up to a door, read what the room can touch, and step in.

Every room is a plain package: a room.json that declares its door, checked by a validator that refuses a bad or sneaky room before it ever runs. An in-app build mode for adding your own rooms is on the roadmap. It is not in the app yet.

05 · Questions people actually ask

Short answers.
No spin.

Is SandboxVille part of Anthropic or the Model Context Protocol project?

No. SandboxVille is an independent project. We are not affiliated with, endorsed by, or a part of Anthropic or the Model Context Protocol project.

Is "51 / 51 blocked" an independent audit?

No. That is our own test suite, run and logged by us, and we say "in our own tests" every time for exactly that reason. An outside security review happens before public launch.

Can I download SandboxVille right now?

Not yet. Nothing here runs on your machine today. Join the Discord to follow the build, room by room, and we'll say so the moment anything ships.

Does the jar really run a fruit fly's brain?

Partly, and we're exact about which part. A real FlyWire feeding circuit decides when the fly eats. Its walking is a simple rule, for now.

Does The Desk trade with real money?

No. It's paper trades only, graded in the open, and there is no buy button in the room. Nothing here is financial advice.

What do I get for the $9 guide?

The door-label method: how we write down exactly what an AI setup can touch, in plain English, so you can do the same for your own.
